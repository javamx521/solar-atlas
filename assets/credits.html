An illustrated solar system

Sizes, distances, rotation speeds, moon periods, and lighting are adapted for exploration. “Today” uses an approximate circular orbit model, not an observing ephemeris. Rings, stars, the asteroid belt, the Pluto surface, and asset-error fallbacks are procedural illustrations.
